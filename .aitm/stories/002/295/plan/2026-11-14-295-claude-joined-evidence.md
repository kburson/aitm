## Gate result and bounded next step

In both trials the unchanged public journal path staged two pending claims. A separate writer process replayed both, with the cursor absent and one coverage-gap plus three unreconciled-usage diagnostics in each trial. The monitor has no accepted way to enroll, consume and reverify the three-channel evidence as writer-authorized input. A reviewed carrier design would need to retain the original source binding, protect the launcher-owned pipe and scoped telemetry, bind the provider account to existing operator authority, and revalidate the evidence at reserve and after restart. It must still produce a cursor via the public journal path, preserve genuine diagnostics and fail closed on changed or ambiguous evidence. The scratch result provides a concrete candidate for that design. #295 remains in Refine, #119 remains blocked on #295 and #204 remains open. Epic #2, C01 and production cutover are excluded.
