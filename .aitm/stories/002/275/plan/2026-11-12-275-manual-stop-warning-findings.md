## Operational deployment plan

1. **Shadow on ordinary trunk pushes.** Implement a trusted default-branch consumer with read-only `contents` and `actions` access. Trigger on `workflow_run: completed` and reconcile missed deliveries periodically. Treat the trigger as a wake-up. Fetch the attempt and attempt jobs by provider identity, pin repository and workflow IDs, verify the event and literal target, and re-read the current trunk SHA and latest attempt before disposition. Reject external or self-declared artifacts. Record an authenticated failed job even if its artifact is unavailable. Classify terminal missing evidence independently. Use the #103 push collector, same-attempt Python artifact identity and digest, and replayed gate for green. Initially retain a job summary and sanitized decision artifact in a disposable dry run. Positive selection can continue locally while #103 remains mandatory. [Workflow-run events](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/events-that-trigger-workflows#workflow_run) · [Workflow-run REST endpoints](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/actions/workflow-runs)
2. **Add durable delivery and verify ordering.** Store the latest observed `(workflow run_number, run_attempt)`, exact alert key `(repository ID, workflow ID, literal branch, SHA, run ID, attempt)`, incident version, active warning, nominated repair and eligible recovery in a single versioned record. Apply compare-and-set or an equivalent serial durable transaction. A new red attempt or terminal evidence gap advances the incident version and invalidates repair and recovery. Repeated delivery of the same key may fill diagnostic gaps in the existing warning without opening a second incident. An already verified failure survives later same-attempt changes in reports. A later verified failure for an attempt previously classified as incomplete invalidates its infrastructure-only recovery. A newer observed attempt invalidates older green recovery, even while pending. Losing a response requires readback by alert key before retry. The in-memory prototype does not exercise concurrent writers or ambiguous provider writes.
3. **Pilot one approved notification destination.** Independently review the publisher, then dry-run warning and recovery text using synthetic exact result envelopes. Verify duplicate suppression through actual retry, serialization, and restart, including a lost response. A later active warning should include failed and missing lanes, exact trunk and attempt links, and a request for the operator to pause ordinary trunk work and assign one repair owner. Choose the destination and credentials in that delivery story. A job summary supplies evidence but has no demonstrated operator notification. No alerts were posted in this spike.
4. **Keep repair and recovery manual.** The operator pauses agents or ordinary trunk deliveries and nominates one repair owner and PR on the current incident version. The PR follows normal #103 and review. After the repair lands, require a complete fresh green run admitted after its landing and bound to the repaired current trunk SHA and current incident version. Read back trunk and latest attempt again when the operator acknowledges resume. A missing-evidence-only incident can recover on the same SHA after the operator confirms the infrastructure fix and a fresh full run passes. A verified failure cannot take that path. Background work and non-default-target delivery follow their normal policies unless the operator pauses them. This advisory route has no protection against an agent or another actor disregarding the warning.
5. **Add scheduled validation only after the push path works.** A scheduled full test run needs its own reviewed default-branch admission contract and evidence binding. The current canonical workflow triggers on PR and trunk push. The prototype admitted only trunk push events. Completion triggers also cannot prove early failing-job notification. That would need separately verified job-level intake. Track missed scheduled runs as evidence gaps and keep the manual playbook independent of the scheduler. [Schedule events and delays](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/events-that-trigger-workflows#schedule)
