## Qualification required before approval

1. **Make the backstop recoverable and verify it live.** Observe genuine on-time protected-default runs across separate scheduled slots, then rehearse the missed, late, pending, red and unavailable evidence paths on a disposable target. Prove one independently triggered, explicitly eligible recovery route for the current protected trunk SHA, with authenticated event, controller, attempt, full outcomes, freshness and artifact ownership. An external scheduler or a separate operator recovery dispatch are options to qualify. The current diagnostic dispatch remains diagnostic. A missing run is incomplete and prompts operator triage and ordinary full validation. A known authenticated regression activates the human hold. Late, stale, previous-SHA or superseded runs cannot release it. After repair, require a new complete green on the current landed SHA and a distinct operator acknowledgment.
2. **Keep the authority boundary testable.** Rehearse the native trunk-only hold with the intended production-equivalent rules and identities. The designated human controls credentials unavailable to ordinary agents. A separate hold ruleset grants only that role a PR-only bypass and leaves normal review and merge checks in force. Prove that ordinary actor push, merge and hold-rule edit are refused, the operator's direct push and a red repair are refused, and unheld work can continue. Record one nominated PR, current base and head/tree, accepting independent review and ordinary gate evidence before the operator uses the bypass. Confirm that the full gate stays enforced while the hold is active. The live effective rules currently show deletion, force-push and PR protections. Treat governed #103 verification as the existing merge authority and prove any future native required-check integration explicitly. [GitHub ruleset controls](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/configuring-branches-and-merges-in-your-repository/managing-rulesets/available-rules-for-rulesets)
3. **Prove the approved decision end to end in shadow.** The first possible pilot is limited to the #292 Markdown-only diff class and known unchanged executable inputs. Require the exact repository, literal parent, current base/head/synthetic merge/tree, protected verifier and admission source, complete collection reconciliation, mandatory tests with child witnesses, and independently accepted custody. Preserve current file, lint, coverage, wheel smoke and independent review requirements. The pilot proposal must show where each existing requirement is met and which evidence can be reused, with equivalence proved on unchanged inputs. Changes outside the reviewed class or unavailable, untrusted, stale or uncertain selected evidence route the candidate to full #103. This includes executables, tests, fixtures, lockfiles, workflows and control changes. A verified selected failure blocks the candidate and remains in its evidence. Full execution may diagnose that failure, and a green fallback alone cannot override it. Broader TIA selection, including the experimental fixture extension, needs separate exact-candidate qualification. Define and rehearse credential rotation and replay/revocation with the production-equivalent verifier before any pilot activation.
4. **Measure realistic complete PR feedback.** Use candidate-bound paired shadow checks on the current production baseline, with real story examples, negative controls, overlapping PRs and refreshed candidates. Time from the first PR trigger for the candidate lineage until all required evidence for the story test verdict is available. Include queue, admission/dispatch, setup, build, execution, evidence validation, cancellations and retries. Report selected, fallback and background full-run distributions, independent sample counts and misses; use the reviewed under-ten-minute p95 and routine fifteen-minute boundary. Predeclare class mix and sample adequacy before recording results. Any budget exception needs explicit owner disposition. Align the >3,000 workload with representative product/native costs and update it as Core behaviors appear. Also record the observed fallback fraction for the pilot's actual mix. ## Cutover sequence for a future, separately authorized change

1. Freeze the candidate's exact baseline and the #103 restore target. Retain canonical workflow, verifier, policy, file-check tooling, ruleset parameters and branch-ref scope. Open a separate change for the bounded pilot. Include the tested gate and policy diff, status/check publisher and identity, allowed scope, rollback switch and authority model in the request. Obtain an independent review of that exact packet and explicit owner approval for activation. Refresh the evidence and review if the approved diff, trusted control inputs or current parent change.
2. Rehearse the routing and restore procedure on a disposable protected target and then in shadow while the complete #103 path remains authoritative. Stage a mode that defaults to full-only, with a single protected and auditable activation control outside candidate control. Keep the full runner available and regularly exercised. Publish a versioned pilot receipt bound to the exact candidate and route decision. The approved verifier must authenticate the receipt and enforce the accepted scope before it can satisfy any production gate requirement. A missing or untrusted result leaves the candidate waiting for the full path.
3. At the approved activation window, require an authenticated healthy schedule slot or an independently qualified eligible exact-SHA recovery, a clear incident state, and a recent verified full run before changing the protected control. Start with the reviewed Markdown class only. Verify the live control immediately and witness one eligible PR and one fallback under real rules. Watch for selection misses, budget violations, stale inputs, unavailable evidence and role drift. Keep the pilot reversible through the entire observation window. Any scope expansion returns to its own evidence and exact approval.

## Rollback sequence

1. On a missed required outcome, accepted invalid or superseded receipt, lost backstop, control drift or a routine verdict at or above fifteen minutes, stop admitting selected verdicts. Also pause when the agreed observation window exceeds the approved p95 budget. Normal candidate refresh invalidates only the superseded verdict and triggers a new exact check. Use the designated operator and the rehearsed protected full-only control approved with the change. Record the triggering PR or incident, current trunk SHA, policy revision, old verdicts and the actor. Discard selected authority for pending merges. If there is a confirmed trunk regression, use the separate trunk-only hold while restoring validation.
2. Restore and verify the frozen #103 workflow, verifier and policy path and the approved pre-cutover gate enforcement rules. Verify the effective diff and rules against the snapshots. Keep any active incident hold intact. Re-run ordinary full #103 on each pending exact PR candidate and verify one complete full trunk run on the current SHA. If restoring the full-only control cannot itself be verified, stop ordinary merges until the operator restores and proves it. No historic fast green carries across rollback.
3. Maintain any active incident until the nominated repair or infrastructure recovery has authenticated fresh complete exact-SHA evidence and the operator explicitly acknowledges it. Record rollback and recovery receipts. A later activation needs a new independently reviewed evidence set and explicit owner approval.
