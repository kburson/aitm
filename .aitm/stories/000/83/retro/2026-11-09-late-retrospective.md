## Why the children waited in Review

The coordinator used one cumulative integration branch. Independent leaf acceptance was recorded as “reviewed source staged for epic integration” and made that head available as a predecessor for subsequent pickup. The #96 plan directed the team to “Close #83 and its leaves only after all delivery and qualification gates have exact evidence.” The coordinator later identified that decision as too broad. The historical planning closure used the same installed-activation receipt and final source commit for every leaf. That coupled unrelated source closure to #96 and obscured the separate leaf review, delivery and sign-off decisions. [#84 events](../../84/events.jsonl) · [#96 closure instruction](../../96/plan/implementation-plan.md) · [Epic events](../events.jsonl).

For example, planning put #84 in Review at 09:35:15 CST. Its accepted source landed in the combined PR at 16:06:39 CST. GitHub closed it at 17:17:41 CST, more than seven hours after Review and about 71 minutes after source delivery. Some elapsed time included real follow-up work. For example, #86, #87, #88, #89 and #92 returned to earlier states for scoped changes. GitHub closed the children in one sweep from 17:17:41 through 17:18:09 CST and closed the epic at 17:18:11. ## What live qualification and replanning taught us

- **Make ownership explicit at shared boundaries.** The initial ledger review exposed missing cross-leaf record ownership and privacy and atomicity failures. The relationship replan then addressed generic write bypass and UUID ordering. The accounting supplement routed three integration findings back to the owners of Claude tier, Codex quota coverage and rollup precision. U07 waited for exact acceptance of those repairs. [Initial replan](../plan/usage-capture-replan.md) · [Relationship replan](../plan/usage-capture-relationship-replan.md) · [Accounting supplement](../plan/usage-capture-accounting-integration-supplement.md).
- **Bind health to the producer.** A manual collection process could satisfy the initial launch readiness test. The replan assigned #92 the service producer witness, reran #93's exact crash proof and bound #94 readiness to the live service process and its incarnation. #96 then proved actual GUI launchd recovery on the host. [Launch-health replan](../plan/usage-capture-launch-health-replan.md) · [Producer review](../../92/plan/launch-health-source-review.md) · [Installer review](../../94/plan/source-review.md).
- **Exercise actual provider-native shapes before declaring the route qualified.** PR #97's installed release held a real Desktop marker with a terminal LF. After the narrow carrier repair, both fresh Codex orders passed, then the PR #98 release held a real Claude request behind timed startup bookkeeping. Both stops preserved the native inputs and protected release. Each went through a scoped replan and independent review, replay on the repaired bytes, protected merge and fresh installed proof. The first Desktop attempt lacked a pre-poll timestamp and served as diagnosis. The final proof bound distinct immutable captures to the first poll. [Desktop diagnostic](../../96/attachments/desktop-marker-diagnostic.md) · [Desktop replan](../plan/usage-capture-desktop-marker-replan.md) · [Claude diagnostic](../../96/attachments/claude-startup-diagnostic.md) · [Claude startup replan](../plan/usage-capture-claude-startup-replan.md).
- **Preserve failed evidence and require fresh proof after recovery.** Runtime verification rejected unexpected Finder metadata before restart. The first Codex catch-up crossed the stop without durable turn context. The successful retry used a fresh turn in the same stream during a confirmed stop, keeping scope and relationships fixed. Record both attempts and keep unsupported observations Unknown. [Activation summary](../../96/attachments/activation-summary.md).

Confidence is high in the exact protected release and observed qualification window. Provider formats outside the retained proof, requests that cross a stop without context and longer-term runtime behavior have narrower evidence. Confidence in the documented historical workflow is also high because the planning transitions, original coordinator account and GitHub receipts agree about the batch closure and its gaps. The late documents preserve those distinctions and leave the original state history intact.
