[C22 and C30](https://github.com/kburson/aitm/blob/0945941c6c024b6dd621202b2456696ae1df0154/.aitm/stories/000/2/plan/core-implementation-plan.md) require live-attempt ordinary finalization and protect the fenced origin against automatic attachment.

An independent read-only Codex advisory confirmed two failures in that composition. It was performed while the exact candidate was draft. This is manual evidence, with runtime transition and implementation clock both false.

- A second request commits a trip and closes the paused attempt while retaining its unresolved expansion reservation. Both ordinary exact retry and explicit resolution enter the C30 open-attempt guard. The trip decision still requires reconciliation of that reservation before a new accepted attempt. [Trip publication](https://github.com/kburson/aitm/blob/8f1b6a6685176c7544f3e5802e9359cd3503f3a6/packages/aitm-core/src/aitm_core/rules/trips.py#L469) · [Open-attempt guard](https://github.com/kburson/aitm/blob/8f1b6a6685176c7544f3e5802e9359cd3503f3a6/packages/aitm-core/src/aitm_core/rules/expansion.py#L83) · [Trip decision guard](https://github.com/kburson/aitm/blob/8f1b6a6685176c7544f3e5802e9359cd3503f3a6/packages/aitm-core/src/aitm_core/rules/trips.py#L675).
- An invocation can publish its origin allowance reservation before the stop and then acquire a fresh C20 issuer claim after the stop. The issuer's claim transaction and send check cover identity and source without the exact origin attempt fence. [Call from C30](https://github.com/kburson/aitm/blob/8f1b6a6685176c7544f3e5802e9359cd3503f3a6/packages/aitm-core/src/aitm_core/rules/expansion.py#L577-L592) · [C20 claim and send](https://github.com/kburson/aitm/blob/8f1b6a6685176c7544f3e5802e9359cd3503f3a6/packages/aitm-core/src/aitm_core/providers/identity.py#L582-L601).

Two probes against the exact head reproduced the failures in an isolated real Git planning topology with the existing deterministic issuer fixture. The lost-reply probe observed one issue, a closed attempt with no reservation disposition, and refusal from both recovery commands. The order probe interrupted the original invocation after its allowance reservation was published and before C20 reservation. It verified that the published stop was an ancestor of the first claim commit and that the issuer received one send after that stop. Both probes passed as reproductions of the faults. They ran outside the product collection. [Exact probe source](2026-11-28-pr-593-custody-probe.py.txt), SHA-256 `ea4ac0263ae267292886265d0d692b6da08fb31372e5512e6ec0ab40dcf7ae3f`.

The advisory also found that pivot continuation keeps reciprocal links but omits durable continuation attribution for a renewed origin. In-scope C31 follow-up should retain child scope across renewal, use the reconciled attempt's accepted plan generation when checking for a fresh plan, and attribute an expansion-child overrun to the origin and its affected child. The two custody probes above do not cover those observations.

The existing C20 observe-only route can retain a positively observed issue after a lost acknowledgement. It keeps unproven cancellation pending. C30 requires a current paused attempt and its existing no-child or terminal-child resolution resumes the clock. Those contracts leave no supported typed terminal resolution for a closed origin. The accepted C31 file scope excludes the C20 issuer and the existing expansion and attempt evidence contracts. Re-enabling ordinary finalization for a stopped origin would defeat the accepted fence.

A bounded prerequisite should order C20 issuer claims against the exact C30 origin reservation and attempt fence and provide a typed stopped-origin custody route. It should reconcile already-issued identities by observation alone, keep uncertainty pending, and permit local no-child disposition only with positive non-issuance proof. Stop and reconciliation must preserve the closed clock and avoid automatic attachment or a second send. Its indispensable Git proofs are the two authority orderings and exact reconciliation after a lost response. The prerequisite and any new dependency require scope approval and independent review before implementation.
