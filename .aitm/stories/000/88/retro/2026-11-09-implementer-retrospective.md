## Misses and lessons

My initial fixtures missed how Desktop places output before the first enrolled response record and how compaction can leave the display and thread totals at different absolute offsets. Independent review also found stale repeats clearing exclusion state and unreconciled first deltas crossing into the unassigned bucket. The fixes required reconciliation at the first eligible request and comparison of independent deltas through a verified compaction transition. Integration review exposed a missing diagnostic for a real quota window with no verified account. Live #96 then exposed another fixture gap, one terminal ASCII LF on the exact Desktop marker wrapper. The repair accepted precisely that framing, and read-only replay joined the original pending command to its following request. The first live capture also lacked its required pre-poll timestamp, so the operational proof was rerun on the repaired release. I would move the privacy-safe native carrier and compaction cases into initial adapter verification and use the installed route to capture both arrival orders before claiming readiness.
