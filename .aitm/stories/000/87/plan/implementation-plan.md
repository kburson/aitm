## Pickup detail

Implement discovery and scan in the approved sources package, using registered source roots and project checkout/worktree anchors. Keep physical locators, in-memory raw rows and any current-directory text transient. Reuse the authenticated source references, SourceCheckpoint and public ledger APIs. Use matching provider-qualified session pseudonym domains with scopes.session_reference. Distinguish physical incarnation from a proven logical stream and retain a keyed content validation anchor separate from the checkout anchor. Reads must be confined to approved roots before and after open and replacement checks. Verify the descriptor and complete newline boundaries. Do not count copied or overlapping bytes unless the continuity check qualifies the stream and checkpoint.

Frames carry stable logical ordinals and trusted segment context. Add FrameBatch.scope_evidence(boundary) to join a SourceBoundary to exactly one frame by provider, session, logical stream and ordinal and attach its verified checkout anchor. An ambiguous or mismatched match gives no proof. U04/U05 use the scanner ordinal in their unchanged SourceBoundary result. Keep source-root approval separate from project enrollment. A sources-owned CandidateSource.with_enrollment selector accepts a verified U02 enrollment for the matching provider/session/stream, starts at the proven boundary and stops at the next incompatible segment. U08 supplies that selector from accepted project evidence. A boundary-only scan can look ahead but reports a commit frontier before the unresolved candidate segment. U08 cannot advance the durable cursor or provider checkpoint beyond it. After restart and later admission, rescan from that held checkpoint and include the first eligible record. Explicit unrelated segments can advance the sanitized baseline as excluded. Test this two-pass path with a temporary transcript, actual SQLite checkpoint and fresh reopen, no prior or later unrelated usage, and exactly-once eligible replay. Do not edit shared ledger, scopes or types files.

Use the pinned activation cutoff to make pre-cutoff rows baseline-only and retain provable later complete records.
