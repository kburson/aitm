# Core design retrospective through full-file validation

This is an agent-authored retrospective on the design work through this separate validation. It is not #2's Review→Done sign-off: no implementation, release, delivery or epic closure is claimed. The operational planning-completion report and reread-procedure audit serve different purposes and are not substitutes for this retrospective.

## Result and alignment

The design now has a verified fresh full-file native XPR: three rounds, five required findings resolved. It preserves the owner's core decisions—git authority, one owning writer, bounded leaf attempts, no routine human gate, genuine delivery evidence and safe bootstrap/migration. The final review accepted the author's explicit own-document route for decomposed leaves after checking the existing implementation plan, rather than introducing inferred parent-plan acceptance. Global review depth, expansion interpretation, second-trip cap and hosted-audit choices remain open.

The concrete additions close lifecycle holes: kind/graduation and bounded splitting, a typed source-review record with explicit independence and assurance, pivot/reconfiguration proof, typed decomposition with separate estimate provenance, and migration over the actual current corpus. Five round-1 and two round-2 optional clarifications were also adopted. A final nonblocking wording suggestion to generalize the older “Graduation” prose was declined: the plan-entry rule and acceptance already explicitly require the common protocol, and the accepted bytes remain unchanged. The downstream plan must name and test those contracts rather than silently inheriting its older baseline.

## What worked

Explicit worktree-local instructions before native startup, small Read chunks and checking actual returned text made the full-reread requirement observable. All 657, 709 and 726 physical input lines were verified in their respective rounds before finalization. A distinct native Claude reviewer found substantive omissions despite the earlier accepted reviews. Keeping every finding and author rationale made it possible to reject an unnecessary inheritance mechanism while still resolving the underlying lifecycle gap. Portable patches preserve all revisions independently of the temporary branch.

## Planning misses and lessons

Earlier reviews emphasized concurrency and recovery while leaving basic kind/decomposition paths implicit. Core-only source review also lacked the concrete evidence schema needed to make its clock and closure claims testable. Migration scope lagged behind the newly created 70-leaf backlog. Future design checks should walk every actual record shape and every CLI path from source creation to verified closure, alongside adversarial failure cases.

The prior reread instruction was not enough; compliance must be checked before accepting a round. Full retrieval still did not eliminate reviewer misses: the actual leaf-corpus gap was found in round 2, so retrieval and substantive completeness must remain separate claims. Long physical lines can truncate native Read output; bounded formatting and byte-checked coverage belong in review preparation.

One local verification helper briefly checked wrapped paragraph strings instead of physical lines and failed spuriously; the surrounding shell continued to native submit. The corrected physical-line/link/whitespace checks passed before the next reviewer launch. Dependent verification and publication steps must fail closed or run in separate calls. A later native submission rejected repeated finding-ID mentions outside the disposition table; simplifying only the author body resolved that schema check without changing identity, permissions or tool code.

## Confidence and remaining risk

Design confidence: **4/5** for internal consistency and owner alignment after this review. The safety contracts are extensive and unimplemented; confidence in runtime behavior or four-hour leaf estimates cannot come from document acceptance. The implementation-plan validation must allocate every added contract, preserve realistic leaf bounds and prove integration ownership. Production review policy, live provider mechanisms and adoption remain later decisions. Turn durations are qualified elapsed bounds, not measured engagement or throughput evidence.

Python concepts introduced in code: **none yet**. The design anticipates typed models, protocols, pure reducers and transactional persistence; implementation and tutorials must demonstrate them before claiming learning from delivered code.
