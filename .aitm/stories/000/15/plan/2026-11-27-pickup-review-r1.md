CHANGES REQUIRED.

The C04 serialization rule itself holds: older Core requests stay on their current serialization, core operation digests and receipts keep their old form, extension owner and version live on a separate extension receipt, and direct C04 unregistered-slot refusals stay as they are. These four items break the accepted child scope.

1. **Leaf size.** The accepted C14 row is **3 h**. The pickup also inherits a **3.5 agent-hour** leaf scope. Those are different bounds, and the text gives no conversion that makes them the same leaf.

2. **Dependency identity.** The accepted graph is C04, C13, and C08, and the mandatory shared-file predecessors are C08 and C04. The pickup pins #9/#540, #13/#547, and #14/#548, and the boundary names only C08 and C04. **C13 never appears**, and none of those SHAs is bound to a contract id.

3. **C06 retention is narrower than the accepted test.** The boundary keeps opaque bytes for **missing or older** extensions and treats a **missing adapter or capability** as unavailable. The accepted `test_ownership` rule is stronger: **disabled and older** records retain exact bytes, and a **missing action or guard owner** stays unavailable rather than complete. "Missing adapter" does not cover a disabled installed extension or a missing action/guard owner. Replay also says recorded owner, version, and paths are an auditable association. That association must not be a current-registration check that rewrites or drops those retained bytes. Registration changes never rewrite old files, which is the right constraint, but it is not tied to the disabled and action/guard cases.

4. **Registered write authority drops other-owner overlap.** New writes are limited to a registered scoped extension, on a declared path, after the C08 preflight and before the C04 stage and ref update, with Core-reserved surfaces refused and no new external authority from in-process trust. The accepted overlap case is **core and other owners**. The boundary only refuses overlaps among declared globs and Core-reserved surfaces. Two registered extensions claiming the same path is not a refusal. Namespace mismatch is in the accepted transaction-boundary test and is not a named refusal here.

5. **Aggregate proof set is not exact.** #549 stays draft and #15 stays In review, which matches trunk admission. "All six reviewed leaves" is not that set. The text lists five siblings (#14, #28, #233, #498, #499) plus this leaf, and separately lists predecessors #9 and #13. #14 is in both lists. #9 and #13 are not in the sibling list. The gate also says both hosted workflows, independent source SAR, trusted verifier, and branch gates, without names or the five already reviewed trees as the proof members. That proof can be run against the wrong six.
