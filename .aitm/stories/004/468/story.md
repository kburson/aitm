# Make session-owned effort capture and closeout routine in everyday chats

## Outcome

Qualify one lightweight, repeatable path for effort capture and closeout in ordinary AITM Codex Desktop chats. Build on the shipped session store and protected planning publisher and retain the owning chat as the source of assignment.

## Acceptance

- Exercise normal story selection, an owner turn and a supervised delegated turn. Enroll before start or dispatch where the route provides that boundary. Verify that later selection changes preserve original story attribution.
- Prove the normal composer path and document any forwarded route that requires exact retained recovery. Recover the final completed turn, including after a restart or interruption, without asking the owner to switch chats or relay records. Leave ambiguous evidence pending and show coverage gaps.
- Settle and publish a sanitized batch through the routine closeout path. Verify protected readback and no-op replay. Record exact route and version, measured overhead, and remaining manual steps. Obtain independent review of the live evidence.

Reuse short-lived session helpers. Global discovery, an always-on monitor and automatic hook trust changes are outside scope. Claude host and nested-route qualification remain in #466 and #467.
