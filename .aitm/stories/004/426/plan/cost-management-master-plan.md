# AITM cost management master plan

Living research and delivery record for [#426](../story.md). Initial synthesis recorded November 24, 2026. The recommendations below are inputs to the [existing delivery plan for #100](../../../001/100/story.md), [AITM master-plan milestones M3 and M4](../../../000/1/plan/master-plan.md#milestone-delivery-map), and later AIPR design. Use the linked spike reports for tested versions, scope, receipts, and limits.

## Portability and provider boundaries

**Provider-reported usage capture** means reading structured usage from the provider harness the engineer already uses, such as a documented completion stream, hook payload, or exact session and turn lookup. Keep Codex, Claude Code, and Grok Build as the execution environments. AITM ships portable code only, with no project-built native binaries, compiled extensions, or OS-specific collection code. A provider's installed executable is an external dependency of its adapter.

- Target Linux and macOS first and Windows later. Use portable runtime APIs for subprocesses, filesystem paths, time, and structured I/O. Keep shell syntax, POSIX signals, kernel inspection, OS-specific process APIs, and service managers outside the product contract. A platform that lacks a provider surface should return an explicit capability gap.
- Keep providers and versions at the adapter boundary. Each small session-scoped adapter observes a launch or looks up an exact prior turn and then exits. AITM owns the common receipt, assignments and rollups. Prefer supported completion events and exact lookup. Agent-initiated reporting is a fallback.
- Expose small, typed operations where useful over MCP. The backing operation validates identity, scope, and authorization. Model instructions and generic shell access carry no additional authority. Keep provider transcripts and credentials private and publish sanitized evidence references.
- Reuse suitable AITM usage types, journals, pricing inputs, and rollup logic after checking their contracts. Evaluate reuse independently of the existing monitor lifecycle. This recommended path requires neither a replacement harness nor a persistent host monitor.

## Recommended delivery sequence

1. **Common receipt and attribution.** Define a versioned observation with assignment, run, attempt, role, provider, harness/version, authentication or pricing basis, native session and turn or request identity, usage categories, timestamps, evidence reference, coverage, and outcome. Keep assignment mutable only through an explicit attribution event. Use stable observation identity to deduplicate reopened journals and delayed replay. A retry starts a new attempt. Quarantine conflicting evidence and keep a rolled-up parent from counting inclusive child usage twice.
2. **Portable adapters and reports.** Capture assigned headless runs on the three qualified harnesses. Add configured interactive capture and one-shot recovery where supported. Show measured tokens or native units, agent engagement, working wall time, and elapsed time on stories and epics. Preserve complete, partial, pending, and unknown coverage. Carry provider-reported pricing indicators and versioned API-equivalent estimates with their basis. The subscription probes established no actual per-story charges. Add billed amounts only when backed by billing evidence.
3. **Acceptance and integration.** Replay sanitized fixtures for all three harnesses through the same ingest and rollup interfaces. Prove fresh launch, resume, interruption, late completion, immutable story boundaries, overlaps, nested coverage, handoff, and late assignment. Run portable core and adapter tests on Linux and macOS. Qualify actual provider surfaces by platform and version before claiming live coverage. Add Windows qualification once targeted provider routes are available. Preserve AIPR sealed coverage for the two demonstrated routes and expose the Codex gap by capability.
4. **Reconcile with #100.** Use this scope as the recommendation in the #426 research decision. Compare it with #100's accepted monitor, deployment, and cutover obligations before requesting changes to that plan. Keep #100's current authority and release status intact during this research handoff.

A single-provider shop can use the same receipts and rollups with its selected adapter set. Cross-provider teams can combine evidence under the same story and compare coverage without losing provider-specific units or review roles. The alternative of gating all delivery on three sealed reviewer routes delays already-supported story and worker accounting. Consider that gate only for a release that promises all three sealed routes.

## Lessons for a later AIPR redesign

- **Keep the reviewer focused on the artifact.** Let the caller prepare an immutable envelope for the review, round, attempt, artifact digest and assigned role. Use the provider's own headless harness. The #430 lightweight Codex comparison supports testing a smaller interaction contract. It supplied neither sealed authority nor a measured savings ratio.
- **Keep authority in a small review interface.** Prototype narrow join and submit operations with a verified native participant, scoped invitation, explicit round and attempt, and a sealed decision and response digest. The service or tool owning that authority must enforce those checks and return evidence for the exact operation. Keep artifact validation and review acceptance under the existing authoritative review interface until a replacement qualifies. Avoid making an OS-specific lock probe part of the portable caller. If a broker operation is pursued, require it to preserve current ownership and process-incarnation checks across its trust boundary. #434 did not qualify such an operation.
- **Treat usage as a companion receipt.** The reviewer can submit a result while usage is still pending. Allow the companion receipt to settle later against the same immutable attempt, without another model call. Verify the decision-to-turn binding, preserve interrupted usage and conflicting evidence, and replay idempotently. A failed attempt can have a usage receipt while the review remains awaiting a decision.
- **Make launch and recovery explicit.** Publish restart and reconciliation states for ambiguous launch, interruption, live ownership and uncertain liveness. Preserve the fail-closed behavior for ambiguous ownership. Minimize inherited launch environment through portable allowlists and use the provider's verified participant identity. The #430 fixtures exposed both foreign provider identity inheritance and incompatible hook loading.
- **Qualify the redesign separately.** Compare it with existing Claude and Grok routes on the same fixture and pricing basis. Require fresh and resumed rounds, exact reviewer provenance, two sealed decisions, interruption without premature decision, delayed and conflicting replay, and live, dead and unknown ownership checks. For Codex, first qualify a supported path with its sandbox active. Publish capture coverage separately from sealed-review support.
