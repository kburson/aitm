Run on macOS on 2026-11-24 with the pinned versions and identities in [#427's result](../../427/attachments/interactive-results.md). After the initial interactive workload, close each harness. Make a read-only lookup by its exact native identity. Resume the same session interactively, submit one additional synthetic turn outside the assigned interval, close again, and repeat the lookup. Compare numeric records across all checkpoints. Queries initiated no model turns. The expected identity and bounded test workspace were known to the experiment. The first post-close read occurred within three minutes for all harnesses. The latest read was after resume and provides only the short observed retention window in the receipt. Long-term expiration and access across users, machines, or remote sessions were not tested. The first unrelated turn was present without an active assignment. AITM could recover its usage by identity, but could not infer which story owned it. The receipt records the known experiment boundary and native observation times. Assigned turns 2 and 3 are distinct from the unrelated first and resumed turns. In production that boundary would have to be retained or supplied by an independently verified record.
