## Authority determinations

**Restart.** `_install_isolated_profile` sets `_live_profile` in memory on the writer object. Its generation ref is `pseudonymize("isolated-issuer-generation-v1", f"{_run}:{generation}")`, which changes on every run and on every replace. Nothing persists it. So:
- **Cold reload under the same live writer:** existing authority suffices. That means a fresh `ScopeState`, a fresh `Registry.view()` from disk, a fresh `GrokAdapter`, and the prior cursor.
- **A true restart, or a new writer object:** there is no authority, and it must hold.
- **After a regrant:** the generation is new. Existing ordered inbox authority suffices to re-arm, but only under H5.
- **No new prerequisite** is needed.

**Multi-stream ordered controls.** The inbox publication lock, writer lease, receipts and `held_edges` suffice for streams present at the cut, provided the callback is supplied (H7). They give no ordered authority to streams that first appear after the cut, such as resume or fork incarnations (H6).

**H4 – J9 cut ordering is wrong as written**

Two defects:
- "Disable captures the first valid native terminal following the arm" captures a turn wholly started after the arm when the stream was quiescent.
- "Enable … strictly beyond the armed prefix" excludes or holds the first post-enable turn.

Pin the per-stream cut:
- **Stream quiescent at the arm read:** the cut is that read's end offset P. Quiescent means no open hook, prompt or user chunk.
  - Note that `_NativeWalk.read` does not currently hold on an open hook at EOF. The strict path must treat an open hook as an open turn.
- **Otherwise:** the cut is terminal + 1 of the single turn open at P.
  - On enable, that turn is excluded. On disable, it is included.
  - Only this case persists an arm: the prefix-at-P ref plus the open turn's start.
- **The open turn's terminal is held or unverifiable:**
  - The disable fence becomes that turn's start.
  - The enable stays pending.
