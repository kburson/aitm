Order is lease → confirmation → _enable_coverage → lookup (validate/dedupe) or _record_sequence and insert → second confirmation → lease → SQLite commit. If either check fails, roll back and raise the original refusal. _LiveSourceUnavailable with changed/unavailable decision, LedgerError including BoundaryUnavailable and _CaptureSourceLost, OSError and ValueError inside metadata confirmation all mean no event and original refusal.
