## Design

Add `tools/story_dashboard/attribution.py` and `tools/story_dashboard/tests/test_attribution.py`. The only entry point is `resolve_timing(tree: PlanningTree) -> TimingFacts`. Consume #186's admitted timing receipts, sole legacy seed and input gaps. Return a deterministic collection of attributed direct observations (work, exclusions, workflow and wait boundaries, missing or pending evidence), typed story/phase/role/attempt/actor scope, sanitized provenance, scoped gaps, evidence coverage and watermarks, and legacy readback values. Interval bounds are half-open integer UTC milliseconds; recording and evidence-through instants retain their precision. Bounds missing from a receipt remain missing. Preserve recording and occurrence separately. This leaf does not calculate or publish totals.

V1 identity is the pair of receipt project and stable observation ID. The recorder incorporates any source namespace needed for uniqueness into that ID. Compare normalized scope, logical turn, bounds, kind, declared coverage, evidence basis and missing reason. Receipt/batch location, source pointers and recording instants are provenance, independent of occurrence and scope. Identical claims merge their sorted provenance and use the earliest eligible recording as proof of that occurrence. A different scope, actor, role or payload holds all assignments and records an identity conflict for each affected scope. A supersession reference alone cannot establish reassignment or a split. Hold every unsupported superseding receipt, even if its target is absent or foreign. Also hold a referenced v1 timing receipt only when digest, project and subject all match; record the affected scope. Keep unrelated receipts available. Phase, attempt and role remain unknown where unspecified.

Index eligible interval claims by project and actor identity. Sweep half-open endpoints for `agent-turn` across distinct direct stories, including epic direct work. Hold the simultaneous span in every implicated scope, including overlap with a conflicting claim already held in full. Keep unaffected fragments with partial coverage and their original identity and provenance. Raw tool intervals remain available to A03, which qualifies them against retained in-turn work. An unattended tool cannot block another story's turn or a legacy span. Different actors and wait, idle or boundary evidence remain available to A03. A pending wait carries the latest positively evidenced checkpoint separately from earliest replay recording. Only an explicit observation recording timestamp for the pending confirmation or an observed bound supplies that checkpoint. Receipt recording time and snapshot as-of do not establish a still-pending checkpoint. A03 owns interval union, qualified in-turn tool counting, exclusions, elapsed and rollups. Scope coverage describes the resolved inputs and does not establish complete lifecycle coverage.

Decode the digest-pinned #181 bytes only through `PlanningTree.legacy_seed`. The format has ten story entries and 114 direct intervals. Preserve the evidence-through UTC, source and reason. Use a separate legacy identity namespace, using actor/turn plus original bounds to distinguish multiple fragments of one historical turn without using story scope in identity. Phase, role and attempt remain Unknown and coverage partial. Treat historical direct/inclusive totals and counted children only as readback values. Compare v1 agent turns with legacy work in the same story even when actor pseudonyms differ, because correspondence across these formats is unproven. Hold overlapping spans on both sides and keep disjoint spans. Absent or rejected seed bytes produce no legacy observations, and invalid seed data produces a scoped gap. Split, reassignment, verified alias mapping and migration remain in slice C.
