## Proposed Rank 50 pilot

Use one disposable, same-repository macos-26 job for an allowlisted synthetic fixture. Spend up to 3.5 implementation agent-hours and stop at the first missing prerequisite. The existing #103 workflow continues to govern PR validation. This plan prepares a named trial route. It does not provision a runner, authorize a new workflow or dispatch, or supply a positive qualification result.

1. Establish a reviewed immutable controller and fixture revision on a named ephemeral target. Obtain the target authorization called for in the #144 design and independently verify the exact invocation route. The controller owns the catalog, assertions and receipt. The fixture runs in an unprivileged worker without administrator membership, access to controller storage or signing authority, or production credentials. Confirm APFS on the actual test volume and positively check native process capability. That probe establishes capability only. Bind the #144 capability receipt to the named target, isolation boundary, platform, toolchain and lock where applicable, exact fixture source and tree, controller and worker principals, evidence custody, run and attempt. Confirm the controller owns the fixture inputs and evidence channel and the worker cannot modify a benign controller-owned marker. If there is no authorized invocation route or the boundary cannot be verified, stop and record the failed prerequisite.
2. Build the pilot catalog from reviewed declarative cases outside the worker. Bind catalog and policy digests, fixture commit and tree, worker and controller identities, environment, assignment, run and attempt. Reconcile the complete catalog before assignment. Evaluate independently observed behavior from a fixed input for each dispatched case. Full means every applicable obligation in the pilot catalog. Selected means an exact subset with the remainder explicitly unobserved. In each claimed mode, confirm that a correctly shaped worker-authored receipt and a reported pass without independently observed assigned execution are insufficient. Cover a removed catalog entry, an unbound or missing result, failure followed by a missing sibling, and a stale attempt. Independently confirm required native child activity and its coverage for any scope claiming it. The acceptance matrix must include positive and negative observations in both claimed modes.
3. Publish sanitized observations and an independent decision on that exact scope. Record unqualified inputs explicitly. The pilot can support further work on the reviewed catalog only. Claiming the existing #133 workload requires reviewed mapping to its complete required inventory, including dynamically discovered pytest cases and native child obligations, plus separate positive evidence and gate authorization. If that broader contract cannot be met, bring back a scope decision for #146 with the proven boundary and remaining gap.
