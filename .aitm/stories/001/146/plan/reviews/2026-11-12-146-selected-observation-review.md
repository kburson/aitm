## Disposition

Accept selected-mode evidence for the pinned product tree and exact reviewed observer/runner configuration. Accept the live omitted-inventory refusal, worker result intake refusal, and preservation of the actual failure with a missing assigned sibling for their tested scopes. Accept the real stale-attempt check on the independent local controller. The final hosted rerun refusal is correct and supplies no successful hosted old-archive replay. A durable artifact retention or independently attested replay channel must be qualified before relying on one in a production attempt verifier.

These trials do not establish an independent observer for arbitrary candidate Python code, separate missing-shard isolation, or production integration. Reviewed immutable-tree admission is a bounded trust model whose admission, dependency closure, receipt authority, and recertification require separate evidence. Advisory selection and warning with human-directed repair can preserve the existing #103 full gate while avoiding a claim of automatic line enforcement. Keep #146's production capability open and preserve #148 and #267 pickup holds pending reviewed disposition.
