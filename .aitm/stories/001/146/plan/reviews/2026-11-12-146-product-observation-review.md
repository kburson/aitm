## Observation and authority

- **Hosted capability and scoped receipt custody.** The controller checked APFS on the worker's test volume, verified a separate `nobody` principal and checked that it lacked root or admin group membership. A noninteractive elevation probe failed. The controller verified private and staging directory ownership and mode and ran worker read, write and staged-receipt creation probes. It checked the readability and write permissions of named locked inputs. The worker used an explicitly constructed environment. These observations cover the recorded principal, paths and operations. This is partial capability evidence for the named disposable jobs.
- **Direct product process.** The controller invoked `aitm-usage --help` under the worker principal and inspected its exit status and stdout itself. Exit was `0`, stdout included `usage: aitm-usage` and `collect-once`, and stdout SHA-256 was `9bad045d9443508f664acd5985a6f59865adc85c31b054187f1ceaea30b2ce9f`. This independently observes the direct CLI process and the two output checks. The existing pytest obligation also checks `--help` in the output and records a child from inside pytest. The direct probe did not trace that pytest-launched child or measure any source lines. Inventory comes from worker `collect.json`; runtime and child data come from the worker's pytest plugin and worker-side reconciliation, which reads worker-owned coverage data. ## Qualification and next decision

The product trial supplies partial capability evidence for the named macOS/APFS jobs, a successful external CLI probe and a reference comparison. No production acceptance criterion for #146 is satisfied by the product evidence. The full and selected contract still requires an independently established complete obligation inventory, assignment-bound execution and outcomes, independent native child and coverage observations, and negative evidence for refusal and retention when some assigned evidence is missing. The trial does not test same-shape report rejection, silent omission before assignment, a stale attempt or authenticated per-obligation failure alongside a missing sibling. #268's accepted synthetic catalog findings remain limited to its own fixtures. Retain the failed attempts and stop this comparison route. Bring back a reviewed scope decision for #146.
