## Interfaces and identity

Add one private STRICT `claim_revisions` table with key and sequence foreign keys to existing `claims` and `commits`, ordered native `later_ordinal`, exact service-keyed `material` and `predecessor` refs, and one typed payload. Its primary key is `(key, sequence, later_ordinal)`, with a unique `(key, material)` identity. The payload retains normalized usage and revision identity plus exact service-keyed later prefix and source locator/incarnation references. A batch can store several ordered edges for one key at one commit sequence. Latest view uses `(sequence, later_ordinal)` order and prior view at an earlier sequence sees only the prior material.

The v1 first claim stays immutable. Derive its revision-one native fingerprint lazily from the exact decoded normalized usage and canonical key using the delivered `_claude_revision_material` codec. The old claim digest also includes original scope and time basis and cannot substitute. Continue exposing one latest PendingClaim per key with original project, scope, time basis and ClaimEvidence. If revision identity is exposed, append an initial-revision default to preserve existing construction.

Exact initial physical continuity joins the stored owner, source ID, stream, generation and boundary to a unique currently confirmed enabled Registry epoch covering that initial half-open span. Use its `position` or `observed.checkpoint.cursor`, match the current native source/locator/incarnation tuple, and reauthenticate the historical epoch prefix with `_capture_position(..., fixed=historical_cursor)`. V1 cannot prove original raw observation bytes beyond that historical epoch prefix. Require its exact normalized genesis in the current writer-issued edge and do not claim a historical raw witness. When a v2 durable head exists, additionally obtain the fresh predecessor's issued witness, and require that its exact later ordinal and copy-stable row prefix equal the durable head's. This catches a rewrite after the original epoch prefix that leaves material unchanged. A matching filename, ordinal, revision number or normalized digest alone cannot confer continuity.

Keep the adapter's real emission order and the single SQLite batch sequence. A singular reserve of a later edge whose immediate predecessor is absent from durable history stays held. Never implicitly stage the skipped predecessor. A batch evaluates against the current in-transaction head after each actual stage. For known exact replay, validate scope and original keyed observation authority. Exact archived material can replay without rebinding source, but it cannot create a forward head or move the original physical cursor. Existing disputes take precedence over both cases.
