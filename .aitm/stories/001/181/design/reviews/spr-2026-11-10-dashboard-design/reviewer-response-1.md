## Required changes

- **SPR-181-01. Attribution must preserve one source identity across corrections.** Lines 19–25 and 30 make receipt identity a function of normalized source identity *and scope*, with only incompatible payloads under the same identity held. Two recordings of the same source turn under different stories can therefore receive different IDs. If recording-local actor or turn aliases also differ, the actor-overlap check cannot identify the duplication. This also undermines the promise to transition to M07, which carries authenticated scope generation and resolves the route independently of canonical observation identity. Specify a stable project/provider/stream and source-observation key that survives reattribution, with separate scope, revisions, and an explicit proven split lineage when one turn spans scopes. Require a shared stable pseudonym basis or proof of correspondence for overlap checks. Preserve the v0 seed and treat unprovable cross-source overlap as unresolved. Test one source observation replayed with a different story or role and a valid scope correction that moves its contribution exactly once.
- **SPR-181-02. Define the Owner wait measurement for the first snapshot.** Lines 13, 25, 29–34, and 48 promise a dashboard Owner wait scalar and subtraction of the waiting actor's intervals but do not say whether overlapping requests sum or union, whether open requests contribute through the cutoff, or how a completed request with a missing response timestamp differs from an evidenced still-pending request. With two concurrent waiting actors, the display and per-actor Actual can diverge across implementations while satisfying the example. Choose an explicit dashboard basis, such as the union of documented wait intervals within the story, clipped to the snapshot cutoff with pending/partial labeled. Separately apply owner-wait exclusions only to the affected actor and specify what makes zero known. Add a fixture where two waits overlap, a different actor keeps working, and missing evidence remains Unknown.

## Builder check

The two choices above affect data identity and user-visible measurement, so the builder needs them fixed before planning. The v0 seed intervals expose `start`, `end`, actor and turn pseudonyms, source, free-text reason, and milliseconds. They omit typed phase, role, and attempt. The builder can retain those dimensions as Unknown for the seed and surface the original reason as evidence rather than parsing it into authoritative scope. A zero-backfill-coverage case should remain Unknown. The split into local capture, local dashboard, and later product integration is buildable after these decisions. The reviewed seams keep C23 trip qualification, C25 native review evidence, C44 calibration, and M13/M14 publication authority with their existing owners.
