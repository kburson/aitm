# #148 Advisory shard planner

The public Python entry point takes normalized T02 collection bytes, their versioned inventory bytes, independently supplied expected binding, lane identities and inventory digest, exact requested obligation keys, integer-millisecond timing hints and a per-lane cap. It reruns T02 classification under full workload policy and requires byte-identical projection. This detects missing mandatory IDs, malformed or truncated inputs, lane mismatches and policy drift. It trusts the supplied inventory expectation and does not qualify collection.

Split by provider, owning package, profile and platform. Keep each isolation group indivisible. Sort groups by decreasing estimated duration and then group name. Place each group on the lightest shard, preferring fewer groups and then the lower slot in a tie. Unknown durations use the lane's upper median of available requested durations, or a fixed one-second estimate when there are none. Support exact empty selection, at most two shards per lane, deterministic ordering, and reassigning by supplying new estimates or a lower limit. Preserve optional disposition and native child metadata in the inventory and reference its digest in output.

Test full and hosted/local bindings, incompatible lanes, unknown timing, deterministic ties, cross-lane isolation, exact disjoint union, missing IDs, altered artifacts, and invalid limits. Run focused and neighboring TIA tests on pinned Python. Deliver through existing #103 CI, exact-tree verification and independent PR review. The planner has no dispatch or receipt authority.
