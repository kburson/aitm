## Design

`select_usage(tree: PlanningTree, selector: ReportSelector | None) -> UsageView` is a pure standard-library projector. It consumes the A01 tree, eligible events, admitted receipts, and graph. Its only files are `tools/story_dashboard/usage.py` and `tools/story_dashboard/tests/test_usage.py`. #190 owns CLI and serialization, and #191 owns operator capture and board readback. #100 owns monitor/export and rates, and #2 owns Core ingestion. A04 opens no private report, reprices no token amounts and writes nothing.

`ReportSelector` is frozen and hashable with the seven A01 keys `project`, `report_id`, `window_start_utc`, `window_end_utc`, `cutoff_utc`, `rate_card`, and `pricing_basis`. The time fields are aware UTC datetimes. `ReportSelector.from_dict(value: object) -> ReportSelector` accepts only those keys, with safe identities and a nonempty UTC window ending at or before cutoff. `to_dict() -> dict[str, str]` emits UTC text for A05. Valid UTC spellings of the same instant compare equally. Selector absence or a cutoff later than tree as-of produces Unknown for all rows. Other valid reports have no effect.

Index A01 registrations and their matching eligible journal facts by identity and provenance. An admitted selected receipt must carry a verification record with the same named operator as its attributable registration principal, report identity, verified story, raw source digest, exact embedded selector, coverage, and pricing basis. Compare any separately declared receipt source digest to the verified digest. Recompute the compact sorted UTF-8 JSON digest of the admitted direct usage payload and match the verification record. Check the verified cutoff-to-recording chronology. Use the existing registration and its receipt digest as the durable evidence. One selector must resolve to one fixed verified source digest across stories. Competing verified source digests hold that report. Leave the source report private.

Group by direct story and deduplicate a repeated receipt identity. Require a single effective direct receipt. For a same-story, same-selector correction, require the explicit previous digest, the same verified source and nondecreasing recording chronology. Follow a single connected chain to its terminal revision. Dangling or backward links, forks, cycles, competing roots, or unverifiable competing revisions leave the scope Unknown. A conflicting direct receipt stays out of totals. Verified effective receipts retain their receipt digest even when their price is unavailable. A held A01 input is linked to Cost only when its known registration kind and project apply to usage. If that input's report identity is unavailable, retain the scoped gap and qualify a supported subtotal as partial. Timing receipt or state gaps do not qualify Cost.

`UsageView` exposes `selector: ReportSelector | None`, `source_sha256: str | None`, `resolved_receipt_digests: tuple[str, ...]`, and `stories: dict[int, StoryUsage]`. `StoryUsage` exposes `direct: UsageMeasure` and `inclusive: UsageMeasure`. `UsageMeasure` exposes `priced_usd: Decimal | None`, `coverage` (`complete`, `partial` or `unknown`), `basis: str | None`, `evidence_through: datetime | None`, `receipt_digests: tuple[str, ...]`, and `gaps: tuple[Gap, ...]` with A01's story-scoped gap type. Use exact decimals and local precision sufficient for every validated input and the number of distinct addends. A verified partial price is a priced subtotal. Missing or explicitly unknown price produces Unknown, and proven complete zero remains zero. Basis and evidence-through accompany available numeric evidence. Roll up direct rows once for the subject and each distinct validated descendant. Missing descendant cost or graph coverage downgrades an available subtotal, and leaves the value Unknown if no amounts are available. Graph gaps affect inclusive coverage independently of the direct measure. Order stories, digests and gaps deterministically.
