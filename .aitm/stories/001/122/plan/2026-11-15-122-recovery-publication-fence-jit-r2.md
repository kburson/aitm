## Receiver decision contract

- ReceiverDelivery identifies the exact FencedPublication and carries a verified receiver reference, immutable operation key and the SHA-256 of the exact delivery payload. Store only keyed references and digest, never the output bytes. Require canonical typed service references. record_export_intent verifies the current durable fence before writing intent and returns only after the same durable barrier. Replaying the exact intent returns identity. At most one operation key and digest is admitted per fenced-publication identity and receiver. A second key for that pair refuses before opening attempt 1. Reusing an existing receiver/key with other bytes also refuses. Use the canonical compatible view on this path. #123's read-only renderings and local file exports follow their own contracts, and #124 queues changed compatible payloads explicitly. The initial intent opens attempt 1. A returned handle distinguishes a newly durably opened attempt from an already pending handle. Reopening or repeating a pending intent holds the send. Verified absence is scoped to that exact pending attempt and may open one successor attempt for the same receiver, key and bytes. Consume the eligibility durably when the successor intent is created. This prevents an old absence proof from authorizing multiple successors, including after restart. No intent receipt asserts that bytes reached a receiver.
- record_receiver_evidence receives a retained ExportIntent and typed VerifiedReceiverEvidence. Validate the triple and stable intent receipt. Supply a closed receiver verifier at construction, with no default accepting verifier. The verifier independently authenticates the raw receipt against exact receiver, key, digest and an opened attempt. Require verified absence for the exact current pending attempt. A verified acceptance from any durably opened attempt is honored and terminal for the identity triple, including late acceptance after a successor opened. That terminal result allows no further send. A #124 verifier must use a receiver that deduplicates on operation key or permanently rejects the attempt proven absent. Otherwise absence cannot confer retry eligibility. The fence compares the returned typed facts with the durable intent again. The test verifier uses a separate key and a canonical signed fixture. #124 owns provider-specific verification adapters and actual transport. Persist the authenticated evidence digest and accepted, verified-absence, or held disposition. Missing, mismatched or ambiguous evidence stays held. Reconciliation cannot override a conflicting accepted receipt. An accepted receipt permits no resend. A verified absence permits only the same immutable receiver/key/digest, with matching bytes verified by the export caller against the digest. Ensure retries of both intent and recorded outcomes are idempotent. Use an isolated signed fixture for verification. #124 supplies the actual transport adapter separately.
