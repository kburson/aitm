## Receiver decision contract

- ReceiverDelivery identifies the exact FencedPublication and carries a verified receiver reference, immutable operation key and the SHA-256 of the exact delivery payload. Store only keyed references and digest, never the output bytes. Require canonical typed service references. record_export_intent verifies the current durable fence before writing intent and returns only after the same durable barrier. Replaying the exact intent returns identity. Reusing receiver/key with a different digest refuses. The initial intent opens attempt 1. A returned handle distinguishes a newly durably opened attempt from an already pending handle. Reopening or repeating a pending intent holds the send. Verified absence is scoped to that exact pending attempt and may open one successor attempt for the same receiver, key and bytes. Consume the eligibility durably when the successor intent is created. Persist the authenticated evidence digest and accepted, verified-absence, or held disposition. Missing, mismatched or ambiguous evidence stays held.
