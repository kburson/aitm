## Design

Implement `project_timing(tree: PlanningTree, facts: TimingFacts) -> dict[int, StoryTiming]` in `tools/story_dashboard/timing.py`, consuming #186 and #187 data. Project every canonical story using the resolved graph and direct observations. Add only `tools/story_dashboard/tests/test_timing.py` for tests. The projection is deterministic and read-only.

The #190 interface is `StoryTiming` with `direct_actual`, `descendant_actual`, `actual` (inclusive), `working_wall` (inclusive), `direct_owner_wait`, `owner_wait` (inclusive), `all_phase_elapsed`, `implementation_elapsed`, `all_phase_age`, and `implementation_age`. Each is a `DurationMeasure` with `milliseconds: int | None`, `coverage: str` (complete/partial/unknown), `evidence_through: datetime | None`, `bases: tuple[str, ...]`, `sources: tuple[EvidenceReference, ...]`, `reasons: tuple[str, ...]`, and `pending: bool`. Evidence references carry a safe planning path, optional receipt digest, opaque source ref, source digest and original legacy reason. `contributions: tuple[WorkContribution, ...]` lists deduplicated work under the direct story and typed project, actor, phase, role and attempt from `TimingScope`, with each row's milliseconds. `calibration: Calibration` reports eligibility and scoped exclusion reasons bound to the exact estimate source. #190 can serialize these immutable, ordered records without a second fold.

Use half-open integer UTC milliseconds. Union retained work per logical actor and sum actors. Tool observations qualify only inside evidenced work for the same actor, scope and turn and never extend a turn or reintroduce a held span. Subtract bounded owner wait and idle only for the same direct story and logical actor, honoring any evidenced phase, role and attempt on the exclusion. A wait on one story leaves the actor's independently evidenced turn on another story available. Preserve unknown phase, role and attempt. If valid same-actor intervals agree on the story but disagree on a work dimension, count the interval once and expose the disputed dimension as unknown. Working wall time unions all eligible actor spans. Compute descendant and inclusive measures over each distinct validated descendant once, including direct epic work in the inclusive value. Incomplete ancestry or an unmeasured child reduces rollup coverage. Keep the original seed watermark and compare the restricted measured graph with its readback; any changed measured descendant membership is a scoped coverage reason. Preserve upstream conflict sources and reasons.

Owner wait is a calendar union. Admit bounded wait intervals or one unambiguous request and response with matching request pseudonym and story/actor scope. An explicit pending confirmation uses its positive observation checkpoint bounded by as-of; receipt time alone supplies no checkpoint. Preserve missing endpoints and do not replace a confirmed resolution lacking an endpoint with a pending estimate. Incomplete wait evidence can qualify an available subtotal. An empty evidence set has Unknown wait. A numeric zero requires complete coverage. A pending request with its positive checkpoint at the start, or another incomplete zero subtotal, retains its state and evidence with an Unknown amount. Other actors can work during an evidenced wait. Keep each measure's evidence-through based on its own qualified sources.

Elapsed and age use only the row's own workflow evidence. A typed all-phase workflow start supplies kickoff. Use typed delivery evidence or the row's verified workflow closure for all-phase completion. Use the selected implementation attempt's Develop and Done, or explicitly scoped implementation boundaries, for implementation delivery. Do not pair across conflicting attempts, use descendant milestones, or derive all-phase kickoff from Develop, issue creation, or the seed. For a known open story with a valid start, give age through fixed as-of with a pinned evidence-through. Missing completion on a closed or unsupported story remains Unknown. The historical #111 proof is Develop `14:15:31Z` and Done `14:28:25Z`, producing exactly 774,000 ms of implementation elapsed and Unknown all-phase elapsed.

Calibration needs a fixed estimate and complete Actual with matching phase, actor, role, attempt or outcome, and measurement basis. A01 supplies estimate phase, roles and source. Use any explicitly retained matching dimensions at that exact estimate event only if the pinned event supplies them. Otherwise preserve the measure and record why it cannot calibrate. A valid observed subtotal or a matching phase alone cannot establish a calibration pair. Core C44 owns the production qualification contract.
