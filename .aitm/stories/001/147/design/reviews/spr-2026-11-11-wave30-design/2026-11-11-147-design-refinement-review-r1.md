**Verdict: revise and rereview.** One blocking completeness ambiguity and one planning clarification.

1. **Blocking — bind the complete set of required lanes through replay** (design lines 23–29, 33; refinement lines 13–15). The design requires a complete T02 `full` reference and computes a complete row digest, but the point and step contract then fixes a singular lane identity and requires delivery proof for “that scope.” The delivered `full` schema at the pinned source has separate required `product-cli` and `ci-helpers` profile/platform pairs with different owners, and it validates that the actual scope set equals the policy-required set. An exact row digest does not independently prove the scope coverage required of a step's authority. Specify that the expected anchor, target, and every step/review/delivery binding cover the same canonical set of required lane identities and enforce exact coverage, or explicitly constrain the result to an individually identified comparison lane and make completeness across lanes a separate enforced caller requirement. Include a refusal case for a missing or mismatched required lane even when the remaining row delta or unchanged statement is valid for its own lane. This preserves #133's complete applicable inventory and does not require a new evidence publisher.
2. **Planning clarification — freeze the current deterministic control classifier** (design line 15; refinement lines 11–12). The inherited `gate_controls` predicate is concrete. The added phrase “nested recognized CI configuration and discovery filenames” and the set of “import-shadowing controls” should become a finite trusted filename/path rule or a reference to the exact pinned constants plus explicit expansion. In the leaf plan, prove a nested positive case and an unrelated product-only case to verify the boundary and retain the two-file estimate. The candidate must not select the rule set.
