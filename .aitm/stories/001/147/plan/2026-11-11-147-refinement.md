# #147 refinement — Bind gate changes and inventory dispositions

Rank 30 · P1 · M · 3.5 implementation agent-hours · [Story](../story.md) · [Refinement intake](2026-11-11-147-refinement-draft.md) · [Leaf design](../design/2026-11-11-147-bind-gate-changes-design.md)

The trusted CI planner needs an exact disposition for gate-defining changes and a way to reconcile earlier full inventory with the current protected-target obligation scope. Otherwise a change to the gate can exempt itself, or an omitted test can disappear from the comparison when the latest full collection is unavailable.

The published [#133 T10 section](https://github.com/kburson/aitm/blob/dbe0d68b3e7b977c9fbf51e26d9ae8c6f629af84/.aitm/stories/001/133/plan/test-impact-analysis-implementation-plan.md#L320) owns `tools/ci/tia/controls.py` and `tools/ci/tests/test_tia_controls.py`. [#139/T02](../../139/story.md) and [#140/T03](../../140/story.md) are closed-completed and Done. At the inspected source baseline `56266ed30bbc572b7e56bc64777e802be264c23f`, tree `8028f12297fbb3ce6012ad238e2242cfd2f2e839`, #139's PR #197 is landed and #140's PR #184 merge `a495d60a87ac4ca764f019dc266e579f13f1787e` is an ancestor. Use the #103 owner disposition and independent review procedure. Keep source and live dependency rechecks in JIT.

| Acceptance | Required observation |
| --- | --- |
| `test_control_edit_requires_full_and_independent_disposition` | A product-only edit adds no T10 full requirement. A changed workflow, policy, verifier, selector or discovery control requires full validation. Exercise a nested exact CI configuration basename and an unrelated product module in the same directory. An addition, deletion, raw-byte rename crossing the control boundary, mode change, or policy change preserves exact old and new identity where present. Missing owner or independent approval holds, including after a passing run. Both exact approvals remove the T10 disposition hold and retain full. |
| Exact control binding | A control change cannot narrow the trusted rules used to classify that same diff. Replaying a disposition across repository, target, base, PR/head, tested source/tree, control diff or execution scope holds. A candidate-supplied tree, receipt or status has no authority. An advisory, contradictory or incomplete pair cannot authorize hosted validation. A rerun needs current applicable execution evidence. |
| `test_reference_inventory_requires_intervening_dispositions` | Given a complete independently qualified A and trusted protected-target history A → B → C, apply the exact reviewed before and after rows and any positively verified unchanged step. Reconcile every required lane and compare the resulting complete ordered rows with the trusted endpoint digests. The result identifies A's actual collection and the C comparison target. |
| Ordered and scoped replay | Hold for advisory, partial or derived references, a stale or wrong-scope step, a missing or extra step, duplicate, reordered, conflicting or unverified history, undeclared changes, a wrong delivered mapping, or a row-digest mismatch. An exact row digest and a valid review or unchanged statement for one lane cannot cover a missing or mismatched required lane. A net-zero change across revisions still needs every intervening disposition or a positively established unchanged interval. Preserve fixed mandatory, child and skip obligations. |
| Evidence boundaries | The derived inventory is comparison data. Ordinary and selected PR paths retain exact-base outcomes. The scheduled and repair paths retain independent required collection, runtime outcomes and full gate checks applicable to their modes. T12, T14, T15, T24 and T34 own integration. |
