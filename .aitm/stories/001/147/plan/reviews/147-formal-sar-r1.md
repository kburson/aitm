An added product row can therefore enter the applicable inventory under unchanged policy hashes even if its source module is absent from the suite policy or its suite and isolation group contradict the policy. A genuine T02 reference does not prevent this. T02 checks module registration and derives suite, isolation, capabilities, mandatory status, skip permission and child proofs from reviewed policy. The successor row digest and exact review/delivery bindings currently establish equality to the supplied row, with no reconciliation against those policy rules. The accepted leaf requires a policy-consistent successor and holds on malformed rows.
